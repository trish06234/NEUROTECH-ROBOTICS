Introduction to Robotics Workshop

Track: Educational

Who is hosting: NeuroTech Robotics in partnership with local secondary schools.

What attendees will gain: Hands‑on experience with microcontrollers (Arduino), basic sensors, and a guided build of a simple line‑following robot. Attendees leave with a starter kit checklist and lesson plan to continue learning.

Time of event: 20 June 2026

Agritech Hackathon

Track: Agricultural

Who is hosting: NeuroTech Robotics with mentorship from AgriTech partners and university researchers.

What attendees will gain: Rapid prototyping skills, exposure to NDVI sensing and IoT telemetry, team experience in pitching solutions to smallholder farming problems, and potential pilot opportunities with local farms.

Time of event: 15 August 2026

Healthcare Robotics Seminar

Track: Medicinal

Who is hosting: NeuroTech Robotics with guest speakers from healthcare NGOs and biomedical engineering departments.

What attendees will gain: Understanding of assistive robotics use cases, design constraints for low‑resource clinics, and a roadmap for building telepresence and rehabilitation prototypes suitable for local clinics.

Time of event: 05 September 2026

Industrial Automation Lab

Track: Industrial

Who is hosting: NeuroTech Robotics with industry partners and technical trainers.

What attendees will gain: Practical exposure to PLC basics, motor control, and a SCARA arm demo. Participants will learn safety practices, basic ladder logic concepts and how to prototype simple automation flows.
